crest

Anki and FSRS schedule reviews to hold your recall steady forever. Before an exam you want something different: the most cards recallable on one particular day, within the hours you actually have. This works out which cards to review on which days to maximise that — and proves how close to the best possible plan its answer is.

Drop your collection CSV here, or click to choose a file.
